ABOUT INAMIGOS

Working together to create meaningful change.

InAmigos Foundation is a Chhattisgarh-based non-profit organization focused on creating an inclusive and sustainable society.

We work directly with communities to understand their challenges and develop initiatives that create long-term impact rather than temporary solutions.

  • Community-focused initiatives
  • Education and skill development
  • Women's empowerment
  • Animal welfare and environmental protection
